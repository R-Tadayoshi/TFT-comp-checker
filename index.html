How the numbers work

Each unit starts from its real base stats for the chosen star level (×1.8 Health and ×1.5 Attack Damage per star). Items add their flat stats on top: Attack Damage and Attack Speed are percentages of the unit's own base values, Ability Power starts at 100.

Score compares a build with the same unit holding nothing. It blends auto-attack damage, ability output (casts in 20 seconds × damage scaling) and effective Health, weighted by the role you pick. AD carry and AP caster mostly count damage, Tank mostly counts survivability.

Not modelled: conditional item effects (stacking, shields, triggers), trait bonuses, augments, and special trait rules such as Avatar or Apex Predator. Crit chance is capped at 100%. Use the score to shortlist items, then read each item's own text.

Data comes from CommunityDragon. This is a fan project and is not endorsed by Riot Games.
